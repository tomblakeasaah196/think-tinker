1. Strategic Overview

The Think & Tinker website is not merely a marketing page. It is the digital operating system for the entire business. It serves as the public-facing storefront that builds credibility and converts visitors into clients. It is also the operational backbone where parents onboard, sign contracts, pay invoices, and track their children's progress.

The website must accomplish one overarching strategic goal: make Think & Tinker the most professional, trusted, and comprehensive educational services brand in Lagos, at first glance. Every design decision, every piece of copy, and every user journey will reinforce this goal.

3. Parent / Guardian Portal

This portal is where trust is built through transparency. It manages the ongoing relationship between the brand and the family, ensuring parents feel continually informed and in control.

3.1 The Automated Onboarding Flow

  • Initial Inquiry: Parent submits a short inquiry form online.
  • Automated Scheduling: Parent receives an automated WhatsApp/email with available consultation slots to book a time.
  • Digital Admission: Parent receives a link to a digital admission form to capture child details, medical notes, and consents.
  • Electronic Contract: A digital service contract is generated for the parent to sign electronically.
  • Invoice & Account Creation: An invoice is automatically generated. The parent pays online, and their secure portal account is instantly created.

3.2 The Parent Dashboard

The central hub for parents. It displays upcoming sessions, a progress overview, recent tutor notes, payment history, downloadable receipts, and quick links directly to the bookstore for supplementary materials.

3.3 Session Notes & Progress Tracking

After a session, the tutor records a quick voice memo. The system utilizes automated transcription to type it out and posts it to the parent’s dashboard alongside topics covered and visual progress indicators (Not Started, In Progress, Mastered).

3.4 Document Vault & Communication

A secure, encrypted area for parents to access signed contracts, invoices, progress reports, and a direct messaging system to reach their specific tutor or Think & Tinker administration.

7. Information We Track (Data Models)

The system will securely store and organize the following vital business information:

  • Client & Child Profiles: Contact details, medical notes, current schools, and active services.
  • Session History: Dates, assigned tutors, topics covered, and audio/text notes.
  • Contracts & Billing: Digital signatures, payment statuses, and detailed line-item invoices.
  • Business Expenses: Categorized spending with digital receipt uploads.
  • Staff Records: Employment details, salary information, and guarantor data.

8. User Roles & Access Levels

Security is paramount. Users only see what they are supposed to see based on strict permissions.

  • Admins (Janeth/Managers): Full access to financials, all clients, all staff, and master system settings.
  • Tutors: Can only see their assigned students, their own schedule, and write notes for their own specific sessions.
  • Parents: Can only access their own child's progress, their specific invoices, and their direct messages.

9. The Admin Dashboard Experience

Designed for Janeth to understand the absolute health of her business in under 60 seconds every morning.

  • Key Metrics: Instant views of Total Revenue, Expenses, Net Margin, Active Clients, and Pending Invoices.
  • Activity Feed: A live timeline showing new inquiries, signed contracts, completed sessions, and new bookstore orders.
  • Quick Actions: One-click buttons to create an invoice, record an expense, or schedule a session.
  • Financial Reports: Auto-generated profit and loss statements and cash flow projections.

10. Phased Rollout Plan

To ensure a smooth launch, we will build and release the platform in strategic phases to minimize risk and allow for user adoption.

Phase 1 — Foundation (Week 1): Launch the public website, digital inquiry forms, online contracts, basic online payments, and the initial Admin dashboard.
Phase 2 — Portals & Commerce (Week 2): Launch the Parent Portal (with voice-to-text session notes) and the Online Bookstore.
Phase 3 — Ecosystem Expansion (Week 3): Launch the Teacher and School Portals, advanced reporting, and automated email marketing.
Phase 4 — Scale & Optimise (Week 4): Refine features based on feedback, add advanced inventory management, and prepare systems for multiple business locations.

11. Security, Privacy & Search Optimization

  • Search Engine Optimization (SEO): The site will be architecturally built to rank high on Google for high-intent terms like "private tutor for kids Lagos" and "STEM club for kids Lagos."
  • Data Protection: All personal data is protected with bank-grade encryption, fully compliant with Nigerian Data Protection Regulations (NDPR).
  • Payment Security: No credit card data is stored on your servers; all transactions are handled safely through a trusted, compliant gateway (Paystack).
  • Child Safety: Strict digital access controls ensure parent data is siloed, and explicit digital consents are captured for any photography or recordings.
