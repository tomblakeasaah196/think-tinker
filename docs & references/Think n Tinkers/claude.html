THINK & TINKER
BRAND STORY & MANIFESTO

The Origin Story

Think & Tinker was born from a deeply personal conviction: that every child deserves more than rote learning. Founded by Janeth Joseph, an educator with years of frontline experience in Lagos classrooms, the brand emerged from the gap between what children need and what the traditional system offers. Janeth saw children who were brilliant but bored, curious but constrained.

She started small—a spiral-bound workbook for one subject, a home lesson for one child. What began as a side passion grew into something the community began to rely on. Today, Think & Tinker operates across five interconnected pillars, serving children aged 1 to 14 and their families.

The Manifesto

"Every child is a thinker. Every child is a tinker. Every child can shine."

We believe learning should feel like play, and play should teach you something. We exist to give every child in Lagos and beyond the tools, the confidence, and the space to think critically, create boldly, and explore fearlessly. We are not just a tutoring service, bookstore, or Saturday club. We are a movement that places the child at the centre of their own learning journey.

Mission & Vision

Mission: To provide accessible, high-quality, and child-centred educational services that build critical thinking, creativity, and confidence in every learner aged 1 to 14.

Vision: To become the most trusted and comprehensive educational support ecosystem in Lagos and across Nigeria, setting the standard for how children learn outside the classroom.

PRODUCT ARCHITECTURE

Five Interconnected Pillars

Think & Tinker's ecosystem is organised into five interconnected pillars. Each serves a distinct purpose but shares the brand's mission: empowering children through structured, play-based, expert-guided learning.

Pillar 1: Consult Booklets

Spiral-bound, subject-specific academic workbooks authored by Janeth Joseph. Each booklet walks a child through an entire subject's curriculum for their grade level, combining scheme-of-work mapping with structured question papers.

Pillar 2: Tutorials (Home Lessons)

Personalised, one-on-one or small-group tutoring sessions delivered at the child's home or online. Sessions are tailored to the child's specific needs, learning pace, and school curriculum. Differentiator: Session documentation via voice recording and transcription, giving parents unprecedented visibility.

Pillar 3: STEM & Reading Club

A Saturday enrichment programme (9:30 AM to 2:00 PM) for children aged 1 to 14. The club combines STEM experiments, creative arts, group reading, and character-building activities in a playful format.

Pillar 4: Educational Consulting

Expert advisory services for parents (learning strategies, school selection), teachers (training workshops), and schools (curriculum design, teacher onboarding).

Pillar 5: Online Bookstore

A curated e-commerce platform for educational materials—grade-specific textbook bundles, branded exercise books, consult booklets, stationery, and supplementary resources.

TYPOGRAPHY

Typeface System

Think & Tinker's typography balances the warmth and playfulness appropriate for a children's education brand with the professionalism required to earn the trust of parents, teachers, and schools.

Primary Typeface — Nunito Sans

A clean, geometric sans-serif with rounded terminals that give it a naturally friendly quality without sacrificing readability. Usage: All body copy, UI elements, navigation, form fields, buttons, and general communication. Weights: Regular (400), SemiBold (600), Bold (700).

Secondary Typeface — Fredoka One

A rounded, bold display typeface that captures the energy and playfulness of childhood. Usage: Hero headlines, club flyers, children-facing materials, certificates, event banners, and primary tagline treatment. Never for body text.

Accent Typeface — Georgia

A classical serif providing editorial depth. Usage: Pull quotes, testimonials, manifesto text, and formal documents (contracts, invoices, certificates).

LOGO VARIANTS & GRID SYSTEM

Logo Variants

To maintain brand integrity across all touchpoints, the logo must only be used in the following approved configurations:

Full Logo (Stacked)

The complete Idea Cluster icon above the wordmark, tagline, and sector badge. Use on official letterheads, website hero header, architectural signage, and formal certificates.

Compact Logo (Wordmark Only)

The "THINK & TINKER" text without the tagline or sector badge. Use when vertical space is constrained but the brand name must be explicitly read.

Icon Only (The Secure Lightbulb)

The lightbulb-gear-book-shield cluster on its own. Use as favicon, app icon, UI loading state, or social media profile picture at small sizes.

Monochrome Variants

Single-color versions in Tinker Teal, Deep Navy, or Pure White for applications where full colour is not possible (embossing, single-color printing).

Mathematical Grid System

The 8px Base Grid

All spatial decisions are built on an 8px base unit. Every measurement in the UI (margins, padding, gaps, component sizes) should be a multiple of 8px: 8, 16, 24, 32, 40, 48, 56, 64px.

Layout Grid

Desktop (1280px+): 12-column grid with 32px gutters, 64px side margins, 1200px max content width.

Tablet (768–1279px): 8-column grid with 24px gutters, 32px side margins.

Mobile (320–767px): 4-column grid with 16px gutters, 16px side margins.

DATA VIZ, ILLUSTRATION & PHOTOGRAPHY

Data Visualization

Chart Colour Sequence: When multiple data series appear, use: Tinker Teal, Spark Orange, Idea Gold, Deep Navy, Leaf Green, Sky Blue, Story Coral. Never exceed seven colours; group remaining categories under "Other" in Cloud Gray.

Progress Indicators: Visualise child progress using radial progress rings (percentage completion), horizontal milestone bars (topics covered out of total), and star/badge systems for achievements. Use a spectrum from Idea Gold (in progress) through Tinker Teal (on track) to Leaf Green (mastered).

Illustration Style

Where illustrations are used (onboarding screens, empty states, error pages, certificates), follow a flat, vector-based style with the brand colour palette. Characters should be diverse—children of various ethnicities, hair textures, and body types, reflecting Lagos's diversity. Scenes should show children actively engaged: reading, building, painting, experimenting, laughing, collaborating.

Photography Principles

Core Principles: Shoot at child level (at the child's eye height, never looking down). Capture action, not poses—children reading, building, mixing colours, laughing, presenting work. Show the environment—colourful, messy, creative spaces with craft materials, books, STEM equipment, outdoor greenery as natural background.

Diverse Representation: Photographs must reflect the diversity of Lagos's children across ethnicity, gender, and ability. Prefer natural lighting; avoid harsh flash. Shoot in well-lit indoor spaces or outdoors during golden hour.

Photo Treatment & Categories

Apply a subtle warm colour grade aligning with Soft Cream and Warm White palette. In hero sections, use a gentle gradient overlay (Warm White at 40–60% opacity) to ensure text legibility.

Image Library Categories: Tutoring (one-on-one sessions), STEM & Reading Club (Saturday sessions, group activities, outdoor play), Events & Ceremonies (certificate ceremonies, parent days), Product Shots (consult booklets, exercise books, stationery), Facility & Environment (physical space, reading corners, activity tables).

Exercise Book Design

Branded exercise books feature the Idea Cluster logo centred at the top, "THINK & TINKER KIDS ACADEMY" wordmark below, subject and grade designation, and organic botanical illustration elements in secondary palette (rust, mustard, teal leaves) on soft cream background. Each subject has a unique colour accent for quick identification.

Digital Templates

Maintain a template library for: social media posts (Instagram grid, stories, reels), WhatsApp broadcast graphics, email newsletter header/footer, invoice and receipt templates, session note templates, and parent progress report templates. All templates must lock logo, typography, and colour usage to brand specifications.

Environmental Branding

If operating from a physical location, environmental branding includes exterior sign (full stacked logo on white background), interior wall decals (brand manifesto and core values), wayfinding signs (using icon system), and branded welcome board (displaying day's schedule and mystery basket theme).

Brand Governance

One master Figma file holds all approved assets—logo files, colour swatches, icon set, type specimens. This is the single source of truth. Quarterly brand reviews ensure app UI, marketing materials, PDF templates, and social media presence adhere to defined standards.
