1. BRAND STORY & MANIFESTO

1. BRAND STORY & MANIFESTO

1.1 The Origin Story

Think & Tinker was born from a deeply personal conviction: that every child deserves more than rote learning. Founded by Janeth Joseph, an educator with years of frontline experience in Lagos classrooms, Think & Tinker emerged from the gap between what children need and what the traditional system offers. Janeth saw children who were brilliant but bored, curious but constrained, creative but confined to textbooks that never asked them to wonder.

She started small. A spiral-bound consult booklet for a single subject. A home lesson for one child who needed extra care. A Saturday morning where kids could read, build, paint, and ask questions without being told to sit still. What began as a side passion alongside her teaching career grew into something the community began to rely on. Parents referred other parents. Schools asked for curriculum guidance. Teachers sought her out for training.

Today, Think & Tinker operates across five interconnected pillars, each designed to nurture the whole child. From its base in Gbagada, Lagos, the brand serves children aged 1 to 14 and their families, offering a complete educational support ecosystem that bridges what happens in school with what should happen everywhere else.

1.2 The Brand Manifesto

“Every child is a thinker. Every child is a tinker. Every child can shine.”

We believe learning should feel like play, and play should teach you something. We believe that a child who reads for joy will read for life. That a child who builds with their hands will one day build the future. That the best education does not happen in silence but in the mess of curiosity, in the noise of discovery, in the wonder of getting it wrong and trying again.

We exist to give every child in Lagos and beyond the tools, the confidence, and the space to think critically, create boldly, and explore fearlessly. We are not just a tutoring service. We are not just a bookstore. We are not just a Saturday club. We are a movement that places the child at the centre of their own learning journey.

We partner with parents who want more for their children. We equip teachers who want to teach better. We advise schools that want to be more than exam factories. And we build resources that make learning personal, practical, and permanent.

This is Think & Tinker. Where curiosity is the curriculum.

1.3 Mission Statement

To provide accessible, high-quality, and child-centred educational services that build critical thinking, creativity, and confidence in every learner aged 1 to 14, through personalised tutoring, structured academic resources, hands-on STEM and reading experiences, expert consultation, and a curated educational marketplace.

To become the most trusted and comprehensive educational support ecosystem in Lagos and across Nigeria, setting the standard for how children learn outside the classroom, and proving that structured play, personalised attention, and expert guidance can transform educational outcomes at scale.

1.5 Core Values

  • Curiosity First: We design every experience to spark questions, not just deliver answers. A child who asks why will always outlearn a child who only memorises what.
  • Whole-Child Development: Academic excellence is only one dimension. We nurture character, confidence, social skills, creativity, and emotional resilience alongside subject mastery.
  • Integrity & Trust: Parents entrust us with their most valuable treasure. We honour that trust with professionalism, transparency, and accountability in every interaction.
  • Play-Based Rigour: We reject the false choice between fun and structure. Our programs are meticulously planned yet feel effortlessly engaging because children learn best when they are joyful.
  • Community & Collaboration: Education is a village effort. We work alongside parents, teachers, and schools as partners, never as replacements.
  • Accessible Excellence: Premium quality does not mean exclusivity. We build systems and resources that can reach every child who needs them, starting with Lagos and scaling across Nigeria.

1.6 Brand Positioning Statement

For parents, guardians, teachers, and schools in Lagos who want a structured, trustworthy, and child-centred approach to education beyond the classroom, Think & Tinker is the comprehensive educational support ecosystem that delivers personalised tutoring, hands-on STEM and reading experiences, expert academic consulting, structured learning resources, and a curated educational marketplace, all under one trusted brand.

Unlike fragmented tutoring services or generic bookshops, Think & Tinker combines professional rigour with playful pedagogy, giving every child aged 1 to 14 the space to think, create, explore, and shine.

2. BRAND VOICE & COPYWRITING

2.1 Voice Personality

Think & Tinker speaks with the warmth of a favourite teacher and the confidence of an educational authority. The brand voice is simultaneously nurturing and knowledgeable, playful and professional. It never talks down to parents or children, but it always makes complex educational concepts feel approachable and actionable.

The Four Dimensions of the Think & Tinker Voice

WARM We speak like a trusted family friend, not a corporate entity. Every message should feel like it comes from someone who genuinely cares about the child.
KNOWLEDGEABLE We demonstrate expertise without arrogance. We cite pedagogy when relevant, reference outcomes, and position ourselves as authorities parents can rely on.
PLAYFUL We embrace the energy of childhood. Our language is vibrant, sometimes cheeky, always optimistic. We use metaphors of building, exploring, and discovering.
EMPOWERING We give parents and children agency. Our language focuses on what the child can become, not what they lack. We celebrate progress, not perfection.

2.2 Tone Variations by Audience (Part 1)

Speaking to Parents/Guardians

  • Tone: Reassuring, professional, partnership-oriented. Parents are making an investment in their child. They need to feel confident. Use language that acknowledges their role as the primary decision-maker and positions Think & Tinker as their expert partner.
  • Example: "Your child’s potential is already there. We simply build the bridge between where they are and where they’re going. With personalised session notes, transparent progress tracking, and a dedicated educator who knows your child by name, you’ll never wonder what’s happening in a Think & Tinker session."

Speaking to Teachers

  • Tone: Collegial, practical, respectful. Teachers are peers. Avoid any language that implies they are inadequate. Position Think & Tinker as a resource that makes their work easier and more effective, not a replacement.
  • Example: "We know the reality of a Lagos classroom. Large class sizes, limited resources, pressure to deliver results. Our consulting and curriculum tools are built by educators who have been in your shoes, designed to give you practical frameworks you can implement on Monday morning."

2.2 Tone Variations by Audience (Part 2)

Speaking to Schools

  • Tone: Strategic, results-oriented, collaborative. Schools think in terms of institutional outcomes, reputation, and compliance. Use language that speaks to measurable improvement and competitive advantage.

  • Example: "Ready to build something amazing? Grab your thinking cap and your tinker tools. Today, you’re the inventor, the storyteller, the explorer. Let’s go!"

2.4 Copywriting Do’s and Don’ts

DO DON'T
Use active, empowering verbs: build, explore, discover, create, shine Use passive or deficit language: struggling, failing, behind, weak
Centre the child as the hero of their story Position Think & Tinker as a rescue service
Acknowledge the parent’s role and intelligence Talk down to parents or imply they’re inadequate
Be specific about outcomes and processes Make vague promises: “we’ll change your child’s life”
Use Nigerian English naturally (e.g. “mummy”, “term”) Use American slang or culturally foreign references
Celebrate small wins and incremental progress Overpromise or guarantee specific grades/results

3. PRODUCT & SERVICE ARCHITECTURE

Think & Tinker’s ecosystem is organised into five interconnected pillars. Each pillar serves a distinct purpose but shares the brand’s common mission: empowering children through structured, play-based, expert-guided learning. Together, they form a complete educational support system that no single competitor in the Lagos market currently offers under one roof.

3.1 Pillar One — Consult Booklets

What It Is: Spiral-bound, subject-specific academic workbooks authored by Janeth Joseph. Each booklet is a self-contained learning companion that walks a child through an entire subject’s curriculum for their grade level, combining scheme-of-work mapping with structured question papers.

Structure of Each Booklet:

  • Cover Page: Subject name, grade level, Think & Tinker branding.
  • Purpose Page: Why the child needs this consult booklet (value proposition for parents).
  • Scheme of Work: Complete topic list aligned to the national/school curriculum for that grade.
  • Author Information: Janeth Joseph’s credentials and Think & Tinker positioning.
  • Introduction: How to use the booklet effectively.
  • Question Papers: Topic-by-topic exercises covering the full subject curriculum.

Target Audience: Parents who want structured at-home academic support; tutors who need curriculum-aligned resources; schools seeking supplementary materials.

Revenue Model: Direct sale (physical and via online bookstore). Can be bundled with tutorial packages for discounted rates.

3.2 Pillar Two — Tutorials (Home Lessons)

What It Is: Personalised, one-on-one or small-group tutoring sessions delivered at the child’s home or online. Each session is tailored to the child’s specific needs, learning pace, and school curriculum.

Service Flow:

  1. Initial consultation with parent/guardian to assess needs and expectations.
  2. Parent completes admission form and signs service provision contract.
  3. Invoice generation and payment processing.
  4. Scheduled sessions with post-session voice-transcribed notes uploaded to parent portal.
  5. Continuous progress tracking visible to parents via dashboard.

Differentiator: Session documentation via voice recording and transcription, giving parents unprecedented visibility into what their child is learning and how they are progressing. This level of transparency is unmatched in the Lagos tutoring market.

4. TYPOGRAPHY ARCHITECTURE

Typography is one of the most powerful tools in the Think & Tinker brand system. The typeface choices must balance two seemingly competing needs: the warmth and playfulness appropriate for a children’s education brand, and the professionalism and credibility required to earn the trust of parents, teachers, and school administrators.

4.1 Nunito Sans
Primary Typeface (Workhorse)

It is a clean, geometric sans-serif with rounded terminals that give it a naturally friendly and approachable quality without sacrificing readability. Performs excellently on screen and in print. Usage: All body copy, UI elements, navigation, form fields, buttons. Regular (400) for body text, SemiBold (600) for emphasis, Bold (700) for headings.

4.2 Fredoka One
Secondary Typeface (Display)

A rounded, bold display typeface that captures the energy and playfulness of childhood. Used sparingly for maximum impact. Usage: Hero headlines on the website, club flyers, children-facing materials, certificates, event banners. Never use for body text or long-form content.

4.3 Georgia
Accent Typeface (Serif)

Provides a classical, editorial counterpoint to the sans-serif primary type. Usage: Pull quotes, parent testimonials, manifesto text, and formal documents such as contracts, invoices, and certificates.

6. LOGO SYSTEM

6. LOGO SYSTEM

6.1 The Combination Mark

The Think & Tinker logo is a highly structured combination mark featuring a central symbol (the Secure Idea Cluster) positioned above a multi-tiered typography layout. The symbol represents illumination and learning, divided into distinct functional zones:

  • The Left Hemisphere (Teal Gear): Represents STEM, engineering, and mechanics.
  • The Right Hemisphere (Orange/Gold Book): Represents literacy, reading, and foundational knowledge.
  • The Core (Navy Shield & Keyhole): Communicates a zero-trust security vibe and symbolizes "unlocking" a child's potential.
  • The Crown (Gold Rays): Five rays representing the "shine" and energy of childhood discovery.

The typography consists of the Primary Wordmark, the Operational Tagline ("LEARN. BUILD. SECURE. LEAD."), and the solid Deep Navy Sector Badge ("EDUCATIONAL FRANCHISE").

6.2 Logo Variants

  • Full Logo (Stacked): The complete Idea Cluster icon above the wordmark, tagline, and sector badge. Use as the primary mark on official documents.
  • Compact Logo (Wordmark Only): The "THINK & TINKER" text without the tagline or sector badge. Use when vertical space is constrained.
  • Icon Only (The Secure Lightbulb): Use as a favicon, app icon, UI loading state, or social media profile picture.
  • Monochrome Variants: Single-color versions in Tinker Teal, Deep Navy, or Pure White.
  • TTKC Watermark: A faded, low-opacity version of the central shield and keyhole used on official documents.

6.3 Spatial Governance

Clear Space Rule: The clear space is defined as the total height of the Deep Navy "&" symbol in the primary wordmark, measured on all four sides of the complete logo. This unit is referred to as "1X". No element may encroach within 1X of the logo’s outer boundary.

Minimum Size: The full stacked logo must never be reproduced smaller than 40mm wide in print or 150px wide on screen to ensure the tagline and sector badge remain legible. Below this threshold, the Icon-Only variant must be used.

Misuse: Stretching or distorting proportions; altering the arrangement of the gear, book, and shield; changing colors outside approved variants; adding drop shadows; or removing the badge in formal contexts.

7. MATHEMATICAL GRID & UI RULES

7. MATHEMATICAL GRID & UI RULES

7.1 The 8px Base Grid

All spatial decisions in the Think & Tinker digital ecosystem are built on an 8px base unit. This creates visual harmony, speeds up design decisions, and ensures consistency across every screen, page, and component. Every measurement in the UI should be a multiple of 8px: 8, 16, 24, 32, 40, 48, 56, 64px. For fine-tuning small elements, a 4px half-grid is permitted.

7.2 Layout Grid

  • Desktop (1280px+): 12-column grid with 32px gutters and 64px side margins. Max content width 1200px. Sidebar occupies 3 columns.
  • Tablet (768px–1279px): 8-column grid with 24px gutters and 32px side margins. Sidebar collapses to drawer.
  • Mobile (320px–767px): 4-column grid with 16px gutters and 16px side margins. Full-width cards.

8.1 Icon System

Think & Tinker uses a custom icon set built on a 24px grid with 2px stroke weight and rounded line caps (Phosphor Icons library recommended). Default state: Charcoal. Active: Tinker Teal. 9. PHOTOGRAPHY & VISUAL DIRECTION

9.1 Photography Principles

Photography is a critical trust-builder. Parents need to see real children in real learning environments. All photography should feel authentic, joyful, and unposed.

  • Shoot at child level: Camera should be at the child’s eye height, communicating respect.
  • Capture action, not poses: Children reading, building, or collaborating. Never line up for shots.
  • Show the environment: Craft materials, STEM equipment, and outdoor greenery as natural backgrounds.
  • Diverse representation: Reflect the diversity of Lagos’s children across ethnicity, gender, and ability.
  • Natural lighting preferred: Avoid harsh flash; utilize golden hour warmth.

9.3 Image Categories & 9.4 Video Direction

The asset library includes: Tutoring, STEM & Reading Club, Events & Ceremonies, Product Shots, and Facility & Environment. Video content should capture authentic action. Short-form (15–60s) for individual moments (Instagram/WhatsApp). Long-form (2–5m) for recaps, parent testimonials, and educational tips from Janeth Joseph.
